About Document Reference Number Generator

DocRefGen generates unique reference numbers for your HR and Legal documents in Google Docs — and keeps a searchable audit log of every document created. Helps you manage your documents better. Designed for small teams that need traceable, auditable records without the overhead of a full DMS.

  • Place {{DOC-REF-NO}} anywhere in your Google Doc template — in the body, header, or footer.
  • When the doc is open, click Generate Ref & Insert in the popup — done.
  • Use it as a generator only, or use it as an audit trail tool too — your choice.
  • Audit trail — add your Google Sheet ID in Settings. Once linked, every time you generate a ref number, one click logs it to your audit sheet. Helps you find any doc later by reference number.
  • Each team can use this independently and maintain their own audit — HR, Finance, Legal, etc.
