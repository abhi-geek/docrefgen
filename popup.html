About DocRefGen

Generates unique reference numbers for your HR and Legal documents in Google Docs — and keeps a searchable audit log of every document created.

How to use
→ Add {{DOC-REF-NO}} to your doc — body, header, or footer
→ Come back here and click Generate Ref & Insert
→ The placeholder is replaced with a unique, traceable reference number
